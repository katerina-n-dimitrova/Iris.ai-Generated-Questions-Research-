MultiHop-RAG — Chunks vs Atomic + Chunk-Level Questions

A closed-collection, 10-article MultiHop-RAG dense-vector pilot comparing original-chunk embeddings (Condition A) with a pooled mixture of atomic-fact and chunk-level synthetic-question embeddings (Condition E). Dense cosine only — no BM25 / sparse / hybrid / rerank.

10-article pilot · seed 42Octen-Embedding-0.6B · dim 1024 · cosine gpt-5.4-mini69 eligible queries k = 1, 3, 4, 5, 10
Headline: Roughly a statistical TIE. Pooling atomic + chunk-level questions and indexing them instead of the chunks left Evidence Recall@5 essentially unchanged (0.489 → 0.475, Δ-0.015, paired p=0.7003). A few metrics still favour the chunk baseline at k=4–5 (see stars) but the primary metric shows no significant difference — with gpt-5.4-mini the mixed-question representation matches plain chunk vectors.

10 · Final report — the 25 questions

1. How were the 10 articles selected?
Query-first greedy (seed 42): shuffle non-null queries, accept a query iff its evidence articles fit a 10-article budget, then keep every fully-covered query. No retrieval used.
2. How many benchmark queries were fully covered?
69 eligible (inference 29 / temporal 23 / comparison 17); 385 excluded.
3. How many chunks?
96 token-chunks (256/50/80).
4. How many atomic facts?
1089 atoms (11.34/chunk).
5. How many raw atomic questions?
1195.
6. How many raw chunk-level questions?
289.
7. How many questions survived filtering?
676 (536 atomic + 140 chunk-level), 7.04/chunk.
8. Main rejection reasons?
confusion-margin 399, round-trip below top-3 320, grounding 8, near-dup 22. (With min-margin 0 the margin gate ≡ rank-1 gate.)
9. Did E improve Evidence Recall@5?
No — essentially tied: 0.489 → 0.475 (Δ-0.015, p=0.7003).
10. Did E improve Hit@k?
Hit@5 0.971 → 0.870 (p=0.029). Baseline is significantly higher at k=[4, 5].
11. Did E improve All-Evidence Hit@k?
E is higher at 4/5 of the k values (All-Evidence Hit@5 0.116 → 0.188, p=0.1489) — directionally better for multi-hop but not significant.
12. Did E improve first-relevant rank or MRR?
First-relevant rank 2.0 → 2.318; MRR@10 0.693 → 0.651 (lower, p=0.4296).
13. Did E improve complete multi-hop retrieval?
13 complete vs 47 partial and 9 no-evidence under E (among multi-hop queries).
14. At which k was the largest difference?
Largest gap is hit@4 (Δ-0.116, p=0.014).
15. How many queries improved / tied / worsened (ER@5)?
14 improved, 37 tied, 18 worsened.
16. Atomic or chunk-level: more gold matches?
Atomic won 63 gold matches vs chunk-level 18 (atomic are ~3.8× as many, so chunk-level is comparably efficient per question).
17. Which type produced more false positives?
Atomic 142 vs chunk-level 122 top-5 false-positive matches.
18. Which benchmark question types benefited most?
See the per-type breakdown — differences are small on this n; no type shows a significant gain.
19. Did chunk-level questions help multi-fact queries?
All-Evidence Hit@5 is higher for E (0.116→0.188, p=0.1489) — directional, not significant.
20. Did atomic questions improve exact-fact retrieval?
Evidence Recall@1 0.197 → 0.204 (p=0.8042) — roughly on par.
21. Did retrieval changes improve final-answer accuracy?
Answer EM about the same: 0.899 → 0.899; F1 0.899 → 0.899.
22. How much larger was the generated-question index?
7.04× (96 → 676 vectors).
23. Additional offline cost?
0.0s atom+question generation (gpt-5.4-mini) + 19.6s filtering + 4.3s question embedding.
24. Online latency difference?
Query embed 43.11ms (shared); search A 1.241ms vs E 4.036ms.
25. Was the improvement worth the extra vectors and cost?
Not clearly — E matches the chunk baseline on the primary metric (ER@5 tie) but needs 7.04× the vectors plus offline generation/filtering, so there is no efficiency win on this pilot.

Method & reproducibility

A and E share the same 10 articles, cleaning, 256/50/80 chunk boundaries, eligible queries, embedding model, cosine metric, k values, gold mapping, answer LLM + prompt, and seed 42. The only difference is the indexed representation: one chunk vector (A) vs pooled atomic + chunk-level question vectors mapped to the parent chunk by max similarity (E). Gold evidence/answers were used only to build the closed collection and evaluation labels. Run with python run_am.py. Isolated from the 15-article experiment.
