The fairest single metric — Evidence Recall@k

Of the unique gold evidence facts each query needs, what fraction landed in the top-k retrieved chunks. It measures the exact research question ("retrieval of gold evidence"), gives multi-hop partial credit, and both methods return the same thing (ranked unique parent chunks). * = statistically significant (p<0.05).

Verdict: On the fair metric, the two are a statistical tie (Evidence Recall@5 0.558 vs 0.554, p=0.87). Generating 10 questions per chunk does not improve gold-evidence retrieval here — and it costs 10.0× more vectors plus offline LLM generation. Not worth it on this pilot.
