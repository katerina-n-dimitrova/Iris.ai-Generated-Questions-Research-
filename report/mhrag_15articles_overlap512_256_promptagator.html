Doc2Query++ pipeline — BERTopic + KeyBERT signals, Iris dual-index fusion

Complete article → BERTopic broader topics → KeyBERT MMR keywords → GPT-5.4-mini 30 grounded diverse questions/article → separate Iris chunk and question indexes → independently min–max normalized score fusion. Every question stores its answer, verbatim evidence, parent article, and evidence-containing parent chunk. BERTopic and KeyBERT use the configured Hugging Face fallback because Iris vectors caused numerical overflow in KeyBERT; retrieval remains fully Iris.
