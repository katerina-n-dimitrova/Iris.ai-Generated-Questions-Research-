MultiHop-RAG — Generated-Question Length Ablation

A controlled comparison of short, medium and long rewrites of the same 239 adaptive fact questions.

Locked controls: Same atomic facts, 239-question allocation, source-fact coverage, supporting 1024-token chunk mappings, 44 chunk embeddings, 17-query untouched test split, 0.5/0.5 dense chunk–question fusion, BM25 chunk retrieval and reciprocal-rank fusion. Only question wording length and its resulting question embedding change.

Generation audit: all 239 short and 239 medium rewrites passed the requested range directly. For the long condition, 214 model rewrites passed directly and 25 persistently short outputs received a flagged, deterministic neutral-context expansion; no answers, facts, or chunk mappings were changed.
