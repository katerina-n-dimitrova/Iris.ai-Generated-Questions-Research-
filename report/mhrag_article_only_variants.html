True article-level retrieval: complete articles are not chunked or embedded as chunks. Deltas are relative to Baseline C.

Retrieval pipeline: 0.5 complete-article cosine + 0.5 best parent-question cosine, combined through RRF with BM25 over complete article text. The returned units are complete articles.
Important metric boundary: Chunk Evidence Recall is not defined in this experiment because no chunks are retrieved. The analogous article-level metrics measure coverage and rank of the gold source articles. These values must not be directly compared numerically with chunk Recall@k from Baseline B or Adaptive Facts B.
