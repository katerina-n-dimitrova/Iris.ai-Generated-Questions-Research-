Why does answer relevance (and faithfulness) often drop with enrichment?

A central finding: enrichment is optimized for the retriever (the embedding), not the generator (the answer model). The same extra text that helps a chunk get found becomes padding in the answer prompt.

  • Diluted context: the original answer-bearing text is now surrounded by titles, keywords, summaries and generated questions, so the model spreads attention and produces broader, less on-target answers.
  • Derived text isn't ground truth: summaries and generated questions are model-written; when the answer leans on them instead of the source, faithfulness drops.
  • Retrieval shifts the context: enrichment changes which chunks land in the top-k, sometimes swapping in lexically strong but less answer-focused chunks.
  • The grader rewards concise, grounded, on-question answers, so any drift is penalized — which is why relevance/faithfulness can fall even as retrieval metrics rise.
  • The exception: when enrichment adds real new information (e.g. the vision method reading a chart), both retrieval and answer quality improve.
