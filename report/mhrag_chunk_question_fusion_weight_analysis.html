MultiHop-RAG — Chunk–Question Fusion Weight Analysis

Controlled fusion-weight selection using the adaptive article-question representation. The five weights were compared only on the development split. The selected weight was then evaluated once on the untouched test split.

Research question: What is the best fusion weight between the 1024-token chunk-vector score and the mapped generated-question-vector score?

Experimental protocol

Interpretation

Balanced fusion is the selected configuration. Giving all weight to question vectors improved development Recall@1 and MRR, but it did not improve the primary Full-evidence@5 metric and trailed W50 on Evidence Recall@5.

Increasing the chunk weight beyond 50% consistently weakened Full-evidence@5, Recall@5 and MRR. Chunk-only W100 was the weakest configuration on the primary metric. This indicates that the generated questions supply important retrieval cues, while retaining a balanced chunk contribution provides the best Evidence Recall@5 among the primary-metric tie.
